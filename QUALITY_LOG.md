# Quality log

Findings from the quality-control passes, whether or not they were fixed. One
entry per fault, newest first. A fault that is logged and not fixed is not a
failure — the standing instruction is to log and move on rather than spend hours
on one thing.

**Dispositions:** `fixed` · `logged` (real, not done yet) · `by design` (looked
wrong, is not) · `watching` (cannot reproduce reliably yet).
