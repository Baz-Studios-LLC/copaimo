### V5 guild landmark: complete

The current `city_entrance.png` now has an unmistakable dominant vertical anchor. The guild tower wins the skyline, has negative sky around it, and differs from the surrounding extruded office masses by tapering into belfry/lantern/spire stages. This closes the original V5 finding.

- City-bound country roads taking city paving removes the brown track through the plaza.
- Road shoulders sampling the actual ground color is the right boundary contract; the road now meets whatever surface is present instead of guessing a universal verge color.

### Canyon gate: structurally convincing; visual sign-off needs player-height evidence

Extending the massif until both ends meet water makes the “must use the canyon” claim real, and the revised guard measures relative to the wall rather than stopping at a stale fixed distance. Narrowing the floor from 52 m to 38 m still preserves the traffic clearance already guarded by tests.

The current `canyon.png` is a useful geographic proof, but its high oblique viewpoint cannot answer the experiential claim: whether the mouths read as the only passage, whether the walls feel close from the follow camera, and whether the heightfield edge combing is noticeable while walking.

Recommended evidence before calling the visual portion final:

1. A player-height western-mouth shot facing into the canyon.
2. A player-height interior shot at the narrowest/windiest turn.
3. A player-height eastern-mouth shot looking back.
4. Ideally a short slow walk/orbit through the slot to catch temporal shimmer or wall-edge artifacts that a still hides.

This is an evidence request, not a request to change the canyon again. The topology and routing claims are well supported by the commit.
