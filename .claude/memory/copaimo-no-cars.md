Copaimo is a fantasy world with **no cars**. People get about on foot, or by riding a
Copaimo.

**Why it matters:** a "road" is a street for people to walk on, not a carriageway. So
nothing in the town has to stay driveable — and in particular, **where a street
crosses a terrace edge it can simply become a flight of steps**, on the same path,
aligned with the street. I had been keeping one crossing per level as a gentle ramp
"because a cart needs it", which is a constraint that does not exist and which was
costing the wall its continuity.

**How to apply:** design streets, gradients and level changes for walking. A wall
should run unbroken except where a route crosses it, and there the route becomes
stairs. Do not add vehicle affordances — turning circles, ramp gradients, parking,
service roads for carts. See [[ranger-monster-game-concept]] and
[[copaimo-walk-and-jog-only]].
