Committed through 715a99d on `main` (local only, NOT pushed). Rebuilds clean from source with 0 refusals — `bash dev/art/animate_ranger.sh`.

Copaimo's character pipeline (built 2026-08-22, all NPCs will reuse it):

- `dev/art/animate_ranger.sh` is the ONE entry point: prepare_rig.py → animate_ranger.py → verify_gait.py, each step refuses rather than writing a bad rig.
- `dev/art/prepare_rig.py` turns the raw Tripo export (`Ranger_Rig_Idle.glb`, project root) into `dev/art/ranger_apose.glb`: drops sphere widgets, mirrors the sides (arrived 5.45 cm apart), lengthens leaf bones to their geometry, strips cross-limb weights ([[dry-no-code-reuse]]: `unfuse.py` is shared, called BEFORE the bake), bakes an A-pose bind (arms 45°, legs 3° out, knees eased 2×4° forward — a dead-straight chain is IK-singular), soles at z=0.
- animate_ranger authors idle/walk/run/sprint from the bind; gait numbers live there per gait: CONTACT (walk 0.46 = one leg; run/sprint 0.46 too), LANDS_AHEAD (walk .5, run .38, sprint .35 — the asymmetric stance is what fits a leg-length sweep inside reach), SWING_LIFT/SHAPE, stance counts 5/3/2 of 8. Spans must be EVEN (a cycle is two identical steps).
- ⭐ROOT CAUSE of five rounds of "still heel walking": the delivered ToeBase ran HORIZONTALLY at ankle height with its head (the roll pivot) at 33-46% of shoe length, 8.4 cm up — so the shoe see-sawed about its arch and no pitch curve could fix it. Anatomy: the ball (1st MTP) is at 70-79% of foot length from the heel, near the sole. prepare_rig's put_the_ball_where_the_shoe_bends() moves it to a shared 72% station (lands 79%/65% — the shoes sit ~4 cm differently on their bones, unsculpted mesh asymmetry) at 33% of the local shoe section height. Must run BEFORE the shoe re-weight, since the weights are assigned by position relative to the ball.
- ⛔ NEVER WELD the mesh. glTF encodes hard edges by SPLITTING vertices; merging them (bmesh remove_doubles) leaves the custom split normals describing a topology that no longer exists, and the character is lit as if it were a different shape — shoes read as shards/melted. Proven by rendering source+weld-only. Every numeric guard passed (edge lengths and dims matched the source to 0.01 cm) because no geometry guard can see shading. And the weld bought nothing: unfuse.cloth_pieces already welds VIRTUALLY (rounds coords, unions by edges) to find garment pieces, and the shoe steps select by position.
- ⛔ DO NOT re-weight the torso off the arms. The delivered skin drives the chest 61% from the upperarm twists and 12% from Spine02, which LOOKS like the reason a spine lean doesn't read — it isn't. Built with and without: head sits +6.0 cm ahead of the hip either way (posture comes from centre_the_skeleton + PELVIS_LEADS). And it shreds the jacket: loose cloth in its own shell, so splitting its weights between arm and spine tears the front panels into triangles — visible at any frame with an arm forward, invisible in side views, and invisible to guards (mine reported "73% spine" while the garment came apart). Boundaries tried and rejected: nearest-bone 22%, narrow core 24%, shoulder-socket 89% but webs the deltoid. Garment weights are a paint-tool decision.
- OLD NOTE, kept for the measurement only: THE DELIVERED SKIN PUTS THE TORSO ON THE ARMS: the chest (110-125 cm band) was 61% UpperarmTwist and only 12% Spine02, so leaning the spine barely moved the body (three lean increases all measured forward and all still read "leaning back") and the silhouette's centreline didn't match the skeleton. unfuse.the_spine_owns_the_torso() now gives it back — 89% spine / 11% arm. PROXIMITY CANNOT DECIDE THIS: in an A-pose the arms hang beside the ribs, so arm bones really are nearest to the chest (median 9.2 cm vs the spine's 18.9) — which is exactly how the generator produced it. The boundary is anatomical: inboard of the shoulder socket + ~6 cm is trunk, outboard is limb.
- Also: 11 bones drive NOTHING in the delivered skin (Root, Hip, Pelvis, and both Thigh/Calf/Upperarm/Forearm) — the TWIST bones carry all the deformation. Don't assume a bone you pose has weights.
- centre_the_skeleton() (prepare_rig, after mirroring): the central bones sat 0.63 cm off their own limb midline and the whole skeleton 1.67 cm off the mesh's middle — visible head-on as "the skeleton is more to the right". Two pure lateral translations; mirroring survives a rigid shift.
- The SKIN must be audited against the bones (segment pitches off sole-ring marker verts, picked by position not weights): the delivered shoes had a shin-owned heel cuff (104 verts on CalfTwist02) and a 50/50 Foot/ToeBase smear across the toe box, so feet pitched in the bones while the visible shoe pivoted on a glued heel. prepare_rig's the_feet_own_their_shoes() transfers shin weights below the ankle to the feet and sharpens the Foot/ToeBase split to a 4 cm band at the ball.
- The ball's authored path is shifted forward by `ball_leads_ankle` (14.3 cm, measured from the bind): the path is for the BALL but the leg hangs from the ANKLE, so without it the leading ankle sat under the hip and its knee folded MORE than the trailing one.
- verify_gait's foot pitch is zeroed on the BIND, not horizontal — the bind's ankle-to-toe line dips 7.4° while the sole is flat, so horizontal-zero refused correct heel strikes.
- HIP_DROPS_AT_MOST is the uprightness knob (hip height IS knee bend, since a planted foot fixes the ankle): 0.024 = 4 cm, which is also real walking's actual vertical hip travel. STANCE_LEG_EXTENDS 0.98.
- Normal walking PEAK HIP FLEXION is 30-35°, not 25 — don't trim the stride against a half-remembered figure. Our thigh swings −23..+30, which is correct. And trimming WALK_CONTACT does NOT reduce the forward extreme (that comes from the swing arc): 0.34→0.30 took 8° off the REAR extension, put the hip ahead of both feet, and cost 11% of the speed. Reverted.
- ⛔ THE "45% LEGS" CLAIM WAS FALSE (corrected 2026-08-23). Measured properly - hip socket to floor, the landmark the human figure uses - his legs are 85.2 cm on 170.2, **50.1% of height**, squarely in the ordinary adult 50-52% band. The 45% figure was the thigh+calf BONE chain, which omits 7.1 cm of ankle plus the foot: a ratio is only a ratio if both ends measure the same thing. It cost hours - the stride was declared leg-limited, a leg-lengthening was proposed and nearly built, and the user rejected it on looks and was right. The real limit was a wrong `covers`. The geometry below still holds: a planted foot pins the hip to a sphere, so reach really does bound the sweep.
- The foot path's `share` is the TRUE duty factor (stance/POSES), never capped at 0.5 — the cap removed DOUBLE SUPPORT from the walk, so at the leading foot's contact the trailing foot was already counted airborne and arced up, folding its knee 80°.
- close_the_loop() copies the LAST frame's keys onto the FIRST after baking (116-120 keys per clip differ). Frame 1 is the loop's first iteration — nothing is keyed yet, so it settles differently from every later frame; the last frame is computed with the action in place and lands on the trend. Symptom was "the left arm is jumping": 8.74 cm of hand travel in one frame vs a 3.89 median.
- The hips ride a SMOOTH cosine double bob fitted UNDER the reach ceiling (bottoming at each contact), not the ceiling itself: clamping frame-by-frame made hip height a step function that collapsed 5.95 cm in ONE frame — the "bounce and jitter". Cap must apply to the FINAL value, not just to the fitted curve, or the hip follows a ceiling that dives to −12 cm. Now: 1.49 cm worst step, 5.55 cm range.
- Trunk lean: he carries a BACKPACK, so upright reads as reclining — reported "leaning back" twice while the number said +1.6 forward. WALK_LEAN=5 (verify_gait allows −0.5..+8 for a walk, still refusing any backward lean).
- The reach limit is a CEILING on hip height, not a target: tracking it exactly made the hip lurch 16 cm in one frame and even rise above its bind pose. Clamp to [bind − HIP_DROPS_AT_MOST(6 cm), bind]; where the ceiling wants more drop, the leading leg just saturates, which is a straight leg at heel strike.
- A 40° trailing-knee fold at toe-off is geometrically IMPOSSIBLE on this character (hip 85.5 cm over a 78 cm leg would need the hip above rest). Deep heel lift raises the ankle and shortens the chord, so toe-off pitch is −22 not −38: 57° fold instead of 80°. Straight-reading leg beats deeper heel lift.
- ⭐"Hips sitting forward / legs don't go back" = THIGH EXTENSION, not hip position. Measure the thigh's angle from vertical (socket→knee): a person swings −20°..+25° and spends ~1/3 of the cycle behind vertical. Ours sat +13..+34 with one frame at −10. Two levers, both pushing the same way: WALK_LANDS_AHEAD (0.48 — more sweep behind, AND less forward reach so the hip rides higher and the leg is straighter) and prepare_rig KNEE_EASE (2.0 not 4.0 — 8° of bind bend seats the knee forward and every stance pose inherits it). Balance point: 0 frames with the hip ahead of BOTH feet, thigh reaching −19.6°. Going to 0.44 overshot (4 bad frames).
- NB a knee ~9.5 cm in front of the hip at mid-stance is geometrically FORCED for any forward-folding knee at 97% extension — real walking has it too. The fault to chase is the TRAILING leg's extension, not "knees in front".
- prepare_rig HOLDS each foot's bind orientation (so the A-pose can't shear the shoes) and then YAWS it about world up to reach TOE_OUT — holding alone meant TOE_OUT was never applied and the feet baked at 17.65° each while the log said 0.0. A guard measures the BAKED heading per side.
- ik_gait.how_the_foot_turns is the ONLY definition of "the rotation from bind to asked" — point_the_foot and foot_roll.ankle_for both call it. They used to differ by the bind's own pitch (7.45°), so the derived ankle didn't match the orientation the foot got and the floor solve absorbed the gap.
- The hip bob's PHASE depends on duty: walk (share>0.5) bottoms at contact (inverted pendulum), run/sprint bottom at mid-stance (spring). Using the walk's phasing everywhere put half the run's dips in flight.
- The limp check compares the two LEGS half a cycle apart (L at frame i vs R at i+half), in cm and degrees. It can't watch hip height — that's a closed-form cosine of phase now, symmetric by construction. Current clips sit at 1.09 cm / 4.05° from the asset's own mesh asymmetry.
- ⛔ NO gather_clips: it accepted a sibling export only if its rest pose matched the prepared rig's, which is deliberately moved away from every raw export — a check that could only fail, importing 3.5 MB per build. Preset clips need a WORLD-SPACE retarget, not a quaternion copy.
- Leg tables hold ONLY the ankle-pitch column (IK solves thigh and knee from the foot path); the authored thigh/knee values live in each row's comment as the design record.
- Build is ~9.7 s: ranger_texture.py skips when the export and script are older than its output, and no longer runs zlib optimise on a dev intermediate.
- verify_gait.py enforces today's fixes as REFUSALS so they can't silently regress: hips ahead of both feet, thigh never extending behind vertical (THIGH_REACHES_BACK), hip bob lurching past what its own amplitude implies (HIP_STEPS_PAST_SMOOTH — must be shape-relative, an absolute cm limit refuses the sprint whose bob legitimately moves 3× a walk's per frame), and the loop seam not closing.
- PELVIS_LEADS = 0.012 (2 cm forward): leaning the spine only rotates the trunk ABOUT the hips, so the hips stay put; carrying the pelvis forward moves the body over its legs, which is what reads as standing straight.
- Clips are authored IN PLACE (hip travel over a cycle = 0.00 cm) because the GAME supplies translation — so in Blender the body stays put and the ground sweeps under the feet, which correctly looks like backward sliding. To judge a clip as the player sees it, render it translating at its measured native speed with the camera riding alongside (scratch probe travelling.py).
- Toes flex at the ball (ik_gait.point_the_foot states the toe segment flat while the heel rises, cap TOES_BEND_UP_TO=50°); the whole-shoe line + flat toes make the foot segment steepen to ~2× the authored pitch, which is correct anatomy. Walk stance window LANDS_AHEAD=0.56 keeps the trailing leg in reach with flat toes.
- The SCULPT's sole was a rocker (only the heel touched; ball 2.7 cm up, toe 6-7) — the character stood on his heels by geometry, unfixable by animation. prepare_rig's flatten_the_soles() (runs AFTER the bake, where feet are flat) pulls the sole shell to a plane HEEL-TO-BALL ONLY, correction fading to zero past the ball — the first version also flattened the sculpted 6-7 cm toe sweep and the user rejected the result ('shoes are just chunky'); the front of the shoe is sculpt character, never touch it. TOE_OUT=0 (feet straight from the leg — 7° read as flare on the oversized shoes).
- ⭐COVERS IS THE MOST CONSEQUENTIAL NUMBER, and it was wrong by up to 36% for weeks. `playback_rate` divides by it, so understating it plays the clip that much too fast - the legs turn at a rate implying more speed than the body has, which is EXACTLY the "running through water" / "Scooby Doo run" reported for weeks and chased through four speed rises, two cadence-band rewrites and a near-miss leg lengthening. Measure with **`dev/art/measure_covers.py`** - NOT verify_gait's `covers_implied_m` (the authored sweep, which the reach solve then clips) and not the old native_speed.py: through stance the contact patch travels back at a constant rate equal to the body's speed, so covers = that rate x span, and the per-frame spread says whether to trust it. Current, all clips 24 frames: motion.rs COVERS **0.970 / 2.502 / 3.298**, player.rs speeds **0.93 / 3.70 / 5.90** m/s, giving 115 / 178 / 213 steps a minute. Re-run the probe and paste the numbers whenever a clip is re-authored.
- The user's re-export replaced walk/run siblings; raw exports can never be copied onto the prepared rig (different rest basis) — gather_clips skips them with a message.

- Speeds are CHOSEN BY FEEL now, not derived. They used to be pinned just under a cadence ceiling taken from human gait bands, so a speed was the OUTPUT of a realism guard and could not be raised without a test refusing it - see [[copaimo-genshin-movement-standard]]. `halfway()` puts a gait handover at the midpoint between two speeds; `CHURNS_BETWEEN` is a wide absurdity bound whose only job is catching a broken `covers`.
- The gait CLIP is chosen from `Striding::wants` (intent, one of three constants) and only SCALED by `Striding::speed` (measured, horizontal, smoothed). Choosing from the measured speed made the warden jitter: it was a 3D distance so it counted terrain climb as ground speed, then crossed a handover ceiling and uncrossed it on consecutive frames, restarting a blend each time.
- ⭐THE BLENDER VIEWER IS PART OF THE BUILD. `animate_ranger.sh` rewrites every existing `gait_watch_*.blend` as its last step, and each scene is stamped with the GLB it came from so the in-blend watcher CAPTIONS THE VIEWPORT - green when current, red STALE when not. This exists because the viewer went two hours and four rounds out of date without either side noticing, and the user reported faults that were already fixed. A stale scene is never broken, only old, so there is nothing to see.
- Ankle angle is the recurring "compressed back foot": leg tables author sole pitch against the FLOOR, right while planted and wrong in swing, where the shank sweeps and the ankle absorbs the difference (+63.5 deg dorsiflexion measured, human range about -25..+30). `keep_the_swing_ankle_honest` measures and corrects it AFTER the bake - the shank does not exist before then - for AIRBORNE feet only, since rotating a planted foot lifts its sole and the floor solve is long past. Hand-subtracting the excess was tried and lasted exactly until the knee moved. The shoe is not deforming in any of this: 0.34 cm lost from 31.7.
- Any translation of `Head` stretches the neck it is attached to - 2.59 cm at rest to 12.61 on the run, +386%, reported as "a long neck". A head bob comes from the hips or not at all; a vertical offset cannot be spent as rotation.
- Sprint constants stated as multiples of the run's have needed re-deriving FOUR times (both arm swings, the lean, the forward arm drive). Each multiplier was compensation for a too-small run value, and against a corrected one asked for absurdities - 34.5 deg of lean, 148 deg of arm swing. If it happens a fifth time, state the sprint outright.
